The Largest Desert

By definition, a desert is determined by low precipitation rather than temperature, which makes Antarctica the largest desert on Earth.

First Computer Bug

In 1947, engineers working on the Harvard Mark II computer found an actual moth stuck in a relay and taped it into their logbook as the first real bug found.

Hotter Than the Sun

A single bolt of lightning can heat the surrounding air to roughly 30,000 degrees Celsius — five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.
